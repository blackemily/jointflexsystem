Food, Nutrition, and General Joint Health

A balanced view of nutrition without treating any food as a cure for arthritis.

Build a sustainable eating pattern

A varied eating pattern with vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, protein sources, and unsaturated fats can support general health. No single food or meal plan has been shown to rebuild damaged cartilage or cure osteoarthritis.

Protein and muscle maintenance

Adequate protein intake helps support muscle maintenance, especially when paired with appropriate activity. Individual needs can differ with kidney disease, other medical conditions, age, and appetite. A dietitian or clinician can help when there are restrictions or nutrition concerns.

Avoid extreme promises

Detox plans, starvation diets, and claims that one ingredient reverses joint damage should be treated skeptically. A healthy eating pattern is part of broad health care, not a substitute for diagnosis, rehabilitation, or appropriate medication.
